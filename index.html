04 · Reference
เปิดกฎเฉพาะตอนที่ต้องใช้Open a rule only when you need it.
01What travels in every handoff›
Object ID/version, decision question, source/date, boundary, truth status, material limitation, allowed use, next safe action and authoritative completion state.
02Shared Landometer vs product-specific›
The shared layer governs method, evidence vocabulary, identity, accessibility and QA. Product data, workflow, scores, models, claims, voice and operational outcomes remain product-specific.
03Cross-city or cross-product comparison›
Compare only under compatible schema, release, boundary, time, unit and method. Otherwise show the incompatibility instead of forcing a conclusion.
04What this page proves—and does not prove›
This page demonstrates editorial hierarchy, official identity assets, role recipes and evidence-bound handoff. It does not certify a live CityMETER result or every artifact-level v0.8.7 release gate.
